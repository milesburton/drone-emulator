Drone Emulator

Drone colour = state (blue on course, orange off course, purple returning, green success, red lost or crashed), numbered magenta flags = waypoints, chequered flag = finish (the pad), faint streaks = wind. Drag to orbit. Hold Shift to look around and fly with W A S D (E up, F down); release to return to the drone.
